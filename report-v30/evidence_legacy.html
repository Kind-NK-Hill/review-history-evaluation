1 What is included, and what is not

The inherited targeted supplement and public-documentation extracts remain byte-identical to the supplied Revision 11 package. The verification/ directory retains the local agent’s bounded handoff, including original records and explicitly labeled context extracts. Its old checks describe that handoff, not the regenerated PDFs. Current document checks are under qa/; the original handoff manifest and source manuscripts are under baseline/.

The bundle does not contain the whole private textbook, the complete historical archive, all role logs or the production database. Original absolute paths inside records remain provenance, not links to accessible files. Model opinions and source interpretations are retained as attributed judgments. Supplying them does not establish mathematical truth, independent expert certification, provider-returned model identity or current production acceptance. No new solver run, experimental model review, Lean/Lake build or statistical resampling was performed for Revision 12.

The experimental materials were supplied for this private review. Their inclusion does not establish permission for public redistribution. The public documentation in Section 5 has its own fixed public source addresses.
